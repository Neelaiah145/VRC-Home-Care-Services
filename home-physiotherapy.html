Overview

Hands-on treatment and guided exercise at home for everyday musculoskeletal problems — back and neck pain, joint stiffness, arthritis and loss of strength. The first session starts with an assessment — history, movement, pain and goals — before any treatment, so the plan fits the patient rather than the diagnosis.
