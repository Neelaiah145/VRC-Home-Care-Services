01

Overview

Nidek BiPAP and BiPAP ST for COPD, chronic respiratory insufficiency and post-ICU or long-term therapy. VRC Home Care Services provides certified hospital-grade respiratory and ICU equipment on flexible monthly rentals and outright purchase throughout Hyderabad and Secunderabad.

Every machine undergoes a thorough 15-point decontamination and calibration check before dispatch. Our biomedical engineers install the unit at the patient's bedside and guide family members through operation and maintenance.

02

What's included

✓ Certified hospital-grade machine with calibrated flow/pressure
✓ Fresh sanitized tubing, patient interface and filters
✓ Bedside doorstep delivery, unboxing and clinical setup
✓ Hands-on caregiver and family operating demonstration
✓ Routine filter replacement and preventive maintenance visits
✓ Immediate emergency machine replacement on technical fault
✓ Zero-hassle security deposit and flexible monthly renewal options
✓ Dedicated equipment coordinator support via direct phone line
