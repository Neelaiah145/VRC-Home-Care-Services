Transparent Pricing

Clear, honest rates with zero hidden charges. Hospital-grade healthcare delivered into the comfort and dignity of your home across Hyderabad & Secunderabad.

✓ No Upfront Payment ✓ Zero Lock-in Contracts ✓ Rates Confirmed After Home Assessment

Direct Billing

All rates are quoted clearly upfront by our clinical management team. No surprise travel fees, registration surcharges, or hidden hourly adjustments.

No Upfront Payment

You never pay before your assigned caregiver or nurse arrives and begins care. Payments are processed only after services have commenced.

Price Transparency

Final rates are confirmed only after our care manager assesses the patient's individual mobility and clinical needs during a home consultation.

Flexible Cancellation

Zero lock-in contracts. You have complete freedom to pause, change shifts, or discontinue services anytime with standard same-day notice.

Across All Hyderabad

Standard pricing applies uniformly across Gachibowli, Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Secunderabad, Kukatpally, and all surrounding residential localities.

Taxes & GST Invoicing

Full compliance with formal GST invoices and digital receipts provided for all services to support medical insurance and corporate reimbursements.

How We Price Each Type of Service

Our transparent pricing model ensures you only pay for the exact level of clinical skill, shift duration, and equipment required.

Service Category Care Scope & Shift Options Pricing Basis
Patient Attendant / Caretaker Trained attendants assisting with daily living activities — bathing, hygiene, feeding, mobility, and continuous observation. Daily shift basis (12-hour or continuous 24-hour residential care). Monthly packages with dedicated replacement support.
Professional Nursing Care Registered GNM / B.Sc nurses providing doctor-aligned clinical medication, injections, IV/IM, vitals, catheterization, and post-op wound care. Per-visit procedure rate (injections, dressings) or full 12-hour/24-hour critical care shifts.
Physiotherapy Licensed physical therapists conducting individualized home sessions for ortho rehab, post-stroke recovery, chest therapy, or senior mobility. Per-session rate (45–60 minutes) or discounted 10/20 session rehabilitation packages.
Palliative & Critical Care Intensive bedside comfort care for bedridden, cancer, tracheostomy, or terminal patients with bed sore prevention and clinical oversight. Specialized 24-hour nursing packages with dedicated clinical supervisor audits.
Medical Equipment Rentals Hospital-grade oxygen concentrators, BiPAP/CPAP, motorized ICU beds, suction machines, and wheelchairs delivered and set up at home. Daily or monthly rental rates with free home installation, technician demo, and 24/7 replacement warranty.
